# Code of conduct

Participants are expected to be precise, respectful and evidence-oriented.

- Critique claims, models and evidence—not people.
- State uncertainty and conflicts explicitly.
- Do not misrepresent experiments, authorship or validation status.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information.
- Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these expectations.

Concerns should be reported privately to the organization owner.
