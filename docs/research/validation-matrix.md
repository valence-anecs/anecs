# Validation matrix

## Primary question

Can an identity-safe, provenance-aware and decision-sensitive economic state reduce B2B coordination cost relative to manual and catalog baselines without degrading decision quality?

## Experimental conditions

| Condition | State available | Action selection | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| B0 | Human-accessible sources | Human expert | Measure current process |
| B1 | Catalog fields and keywords | Conventional filtering | Discovery baseline |
| T1 | Capability and requirement ontology | No DSU policy | Isolate semantic representation |
| T2 | T1 plus identity, provenance, time and conflict | No DSU policy | Isolate epistemic safety |
| T3 | T2 plus Gap, Readiness and DSU | Deterministic P0 | Measure coordination utility |
| T4 | Same state as T3 | Human expert | Compare P0 with expert heuristic |

All conditions must use the same frozen Need, candidate universe and source snapshot.

## Primary measures

- Evaluator relevance at K
- Qualified decision rate
- False qualification rate
- Late-blocker rate
- External contacts
- Human minutes
- Waiting time
- Total interaction count
- Model and infrastructure cost
- Supplier response effort

## Decision gate

A GO recommendation requires T3 to improve interaction or elapsed-time cost against B0 and B1 on at least two frozen Needs without degrading adjudicated decision quality. A demonstration, one evaluator or one successful supplier path is not sufficient.
